Enforce Authenticator App MFA on Business Email Accounts

Switch from SMS text codes to an authenticator application (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, or Bitwarden) for all executive and finance emails.

Step-by-Step Non-Technical Guidance
  1. Log in to your Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 Admin Center using your primary administrator credentials.
  2. Navigate to Security > Authentication > Multi-Factor Methods.
  3. Uncheck "Text Message (SMS)" as an allowed primary authorization factor.
  4. Ensure "Mobile App Authenticator (TOTP)" is enforced as default.
  5. Notify the 12 staff members via internal memo to open their authenticator apps when logging in on Monday morning.
